# One-Star Moon Review — Variants

Competition: **The Moon Gets One Star** (`u-eager-stork-81-9647-the-moon-gets-one-star-768236e5`)
Rule: a single one-star review of the Moon, **max 120 characters**, funniest line in the field.
It must read like a real review: a rating/complaint, and a reason the Moon deserves one star.

Scores are out of 10 per axis (Humour / Real-review feel / Originality), total out of 30.

## V1 — 119 chars — ★ WINNER

> One star. The Moon shows up late, never replies, and just stares all night. Worst roommate ever. Would not orbit again.

- Humour: 9 — "Worst roommate ever. Would not orbit again." is a two-punch close; "orbit" lands the Moon pun without forcing it.
- Real-review feel: 10 — opens with the rating, lists concrete complaints (late, unresponsive, staring), ends with a verdict.
- Originality: 8 — roommate framing is fresh for a celestial body; the pun is earned, not the whole joke.
- **Total: 27/30**

## Why V1 wins

Highest total (27/30) and the only variant scoring 10/10 on real-review feel: it opens
with the star rating, stacks three concrete complaints, and closes with a verdict that
also delivers the joke ("Would not orbit again"). It reads exactly like a one-star
review a frustrated tenant would leave — the Moon just happens to be the tenant's
roommate. V7 has sharper wordplay and V3/V5 tie on originality, but neither sustains
the review voice from first word to last the way V1 does.
